title: "Introduction"
description: "Acme Notes is a shared notebook for small teams."
---

Acme Notes lets your team write, organize, and share notes in one place. Create a notebook, invite teammates, and edit together in real time. Acme Notes supports single sign-on (SSO) so your team can sign in with your company's identity provider.

This is a demo documentation site used to test the Mintlify agent for Microsoft Teams.
